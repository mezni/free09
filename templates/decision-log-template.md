## How to use this log

Use an ADR for material architectural, security, data, or operational
decisions. This log is an index, not a substitute for the ADR.

- One row per decision, in date order.
- The **Decision** column states the decision in one sentence.
- The **Rationale** column states why, in one sentence.
- The **Alternatives** column names the options that were rejected.
- The **Owner** column names the person accountable for the decision.
- The **Related spec/ADR** column names the decision record or the
  specification the decision belongs to.
